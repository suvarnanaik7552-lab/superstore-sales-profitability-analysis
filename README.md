# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales data to identify key drivers of sales, profitability, discounts, customer performance, regional performance, shipping efficiency, and product profitability.

The project was developed using Microsoft Excel and Power Query, with the analysis translated into an interactive Executive Dashboard and management recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this analysis is to understand:

- How sales and profit are performing
- Which categories and products are most profitable
- Where profitability is weak
- How discounts affect profitability
- Which regions perform better
- How customer segments contribute to the business
- How shipping modes affect operations
- How business performance changes over time
- Which products are contributing to losses

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Profitability

The business generated $2.30M in sales and $286.40K in profit, resulting in an overall profit margin of 12.47%.

However, 1,871 transactions, representing 18.72% of transactions, were loss-making.

### 2. Category Performance

Technology was the strongest category in terms of profitability.

- Technology Profit Margin: 17.40%
- Office Supplies Profit Margin: 17.04%
- Furniture Profit Margin: 2.49%

Furniture represents a significant profitability opportunity because it generates substantial sales but has a comparatively low profit margin.

### 3. Discount Impact

Profitability decreases at higher discount levels, with some discount bands becoming loss-making.

This indicates that discount strategies should be reviewed to protect profit margins.

### 4. Regional Performance

Profitability varies across regions.

The West region generated the highest profit, while the Central region generated the lowest profit among the four regions.

### 5. Product Performance

Product profitability varies significantly.

The bottom 10 products generated approximately $120.88K in sales while producing a combined loss of approximately $29.46K.

These products require further investigation based on pricing, discounts, category, and region.

### 6. Time Performance

Sales and profit increased over the analysis period from 2014 to 2017.

2017 recorded the highest annual sales and profit.
